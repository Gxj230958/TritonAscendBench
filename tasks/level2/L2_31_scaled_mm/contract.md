## Required semantics

Compressed-tensors W8A8 scaled GEMM with per-token and per-channel dequant
scales and an optional bias:

```
acc[m, n] = sum_k A[m, k] * B[k, n]          # int8 x int8 -> int32 (exact)
C[m, n]   = scale_a[m] * scale_b[n] * acc    # cast to out_dtype (bf16)
C[m, n]  += bias[n]                          # added after the cast
```

- `A`: `[M, K]` int8 (quantized activations, per-token scale `scale_a[M, 1]`).
- `B`: `[K, N]` int8 (quantized weights, per-channel scale `scale_b[N, 1]`).
- `bias`: `[N]` bf16, added in output dtype after the dequant cast.
- Output `C`: `[M, N]` bf16. out_dtype is bf16 so the dequantized product
  stays in range (bf16 carries fp32's exponent).

The kernel accumulates int8×int8 in int32 over the full K with masked tail
loads, so the result is independent of the launch tiling. Scales are applied
after the dot; bias is added after the output cast.
