### Reference math (oracle helper)

DeepSeek-V3 MLA K assembly. `k_nope` is the per-head nope part
`[B, H, Dn]`; `k_rope` is the SHARED rope part `[B, 1, Dr]` broadcast
across the H heads. For each head `h` the assembled cache vector is the
concatenation cast to the cache dtype:

    k[:, h, :Dn] = k_nope[:, h, :]
    k[:, h, Dn:] = k_rope[:, 0, :]   # same rope slice for every head

`out_dtype` names the cast target (bf16 / fp16 / fp32). The frozen
`@triton.jit` kernel uses `tl.arange(0, H/Dn/Dr)` without masking, so
H, Dn and Dr are powers of two.
